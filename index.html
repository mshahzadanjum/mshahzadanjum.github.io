PROFILE

About me

I am an astrophysicist whose research centres on nonthermal emission from active galactic nuclei and blazars.

My work examines spectral energy distributions, spectral curvature, particle acceleration, and radiation processes in relativistic jets. I am interested in connecting gamma-ray and multiwavelength observations with physical models of energetic astrophysical sources.

Alongside research, I have taught physics at undergraduate and graduate levels and enjoy helping students build strong conceptual and computational foundations.
